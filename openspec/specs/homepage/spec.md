# Homepage

## Purpose

The landing page with hero section, photo, name, title, tagline, and social links.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Hero section
The homepage SHALL display a hero section with the site owner's photo, name, title, and tagline.

#### Scenario: Hero displays all elements
- **WHEN** the homepage loads
- **THEN** it shows a placeholder avatar photo, the name "Hasibur Rahman", the title "Software Developer", and a tagline

### Requirement: Social links on homepage
The homepage SHALL display links to GitHub, LinkedIn, and Facebook in the hero section.

#### Scenario: Social links render
- **WHEN** the homepage loads
- **THEN** clickable links to GitHub, LinkedIn, and Facebook are visible (placeholder URLs)

### Requirement: Minimal design
The homepage SHALL follow a minimal design with ample whitespace and the terminal aesthetic.

#### Scenario: Clean layout
- **WHEN** the homepage renders
- **THEN** the hero section is centered with generous spacing and no clutter
