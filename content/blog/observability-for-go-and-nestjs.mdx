## Overview of Observability

Observability turns runtime signals into actionable understanding. For Go and NestJS services, a unified telemetry strategy reduces mean time to recovery.

Its primary functions include:

- Capturing structured logs with request context.
- Emitting metrics for latency and error rates.
- Tracing cross-service request paths.

### Comparison with Logging-Only Monitoring

Observability requires more than plain logs:

- **Metrics:** Reveal trend and saturation patterns.
- **Tracing:** Explains distributed latency.
- **Structured Logs:** Improve search and correlation.
- **Alerting:** Detects impact before users report it.

Logging-only systems can hide performance and dependency bottlenecks until outages occur.

## Roadmap

Adopt trace IDs everywhere, define service-level indicators, and build actionable alert policies tied to user-impact thresholds.
