## Overview of Team Testing Strategy

A testing strategy should optimize feedback loops while protecting core business flows. Effective teams map test depth to risk and change frequency.

Its primary functions include:

- Catching regressions before deployment.
- Reducing incident rate in high-risk areas.
- Making refactors safer and faster.

### Comparison with Coverage-Only Goals

Coverage percentage alone is not a reliable quality indicator:

- **Behavior Focus:** Meaningful assertions matter more than line count.
- **Risk Alignment:** Critical paths need stronger guarantees.
- **Execution Speed:** Slow suites reduce adoption.
- **Signal Quality:** Flaky tests erode trust.

Coverage is still useful, but it should support strategy rather than define it.

## Roadmap

Classify tests by confidence level, measure flakiness, and prioritize deterministic suites for continuous integration.
