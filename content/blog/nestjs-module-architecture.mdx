## Overview of NestJS Modules

NestJS modules define the shape of a codebase. Strong module boundaries reduce accidental coupling and clarify ownership across teams.

Its primary functions include:

- Grouping controllers, providers, and adapters by feature.
- Controlling dependency exposure through exports.
- Enabling incremental decomposition into bounded contexts.

### Comparison with Folder-Only Organization

Module-first architecture offers stronger guarantees than folder naming conventions alone:

- **Dependency Visibility:** Imports and exports make contracts explicit.
- **Isolation:** Internal providers remain private by default.
- **Refactoring Confidence:** Boundaries reveal impact before changes.
- **Team Collaboration:** Ownership maps to feature modules.

Folder-only organization can look neat but often hides dependency drift and implicit coupling.

## Roadmap

Define module-level contracts, remove cross-feature shortcuts, and introduce domain-specific shared modules only when reuse is truly stable.
