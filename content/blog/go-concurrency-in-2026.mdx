## Overview of Go Concurrency

Go concurrency is still one of the strongest reasons to adopt Go for backend workloads. Goroutines and channels provide lightweight primitives, but production systems require explicit lifecycle and failure design.

Its primary benefits include:

- Efficient request and job processing.
- Clear cancellation via context propagation.
- Composable worker and pipeline patterns.

### Comparison with Traditional Threading

Go concurrency differs from manual thread management in several ways:

- **Scheduling Model:** Goroutines are multiplexed by the Go runtime.
- **Communication Pattern:** Channels encourage message passing over shared state.
- **Cancellation Standards:** Context provides a common control contract.
- **Operational Simplicity:** Less boilerplate for parallel work.

Thread-based architectures can match these outcomes, but they usually need heavier frameworks and stricter discipline to avoid deadlocks, leaks, and race conditions.

## Roadmap

Teams should standardize context usage, define channel ownership patterns, and enforce race detection in CI to scale concurrency safely.
