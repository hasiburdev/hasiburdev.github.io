## Overview of Unit Testing

Unit tests are most valuable when they validate business intent rather than implementation details. They should be fast, isolated, and clear.

Its primary functions include:

- Verifying decision logic under many scenarios.
- Documenting expected behavior for future maintainers.
- Providing rapid feedback during local development.

### Comparison with Mock-Heavy Unit Tests

Behavior-driven unit tests outperform overly mocked tests:

- **Stability:** Less coupling to private internals.
- **Readability:** Scenarios reflect business language.
- **Refactor Safety:** Internal changes break fewer tests.
- **Confidence:** Assertions target outcomes.

Mock-heavy suites can pass while hiding real regressions in business behavior.

## Roadmap

Define domain-level test cases first, then add minimal doubles only for external boundaries.
