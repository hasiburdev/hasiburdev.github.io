## Overview of Guards and Interceptors

Guards and interceptors help enforce cross-cutting concerns without bloating controllers. They are key tools for predictable request handling.

Its primary functions include:

- Protecting endpoints with role and policy checks.
- Instrumenting request flow for logs and metrics.
- Transforming response output into stable client contracts.

### Comparison with Inline Controller Logic

Centralized control in guards and interceptors is superior to inline checks in most APIs:

- **Reusability:** Policies are defined once and reused.
- **Consistency:** Behavior stays uniform across endpoints.
- **Observability:** Interceptors provide a shared instrumentation point.
- **Maintainability:** Controllers remain focused on use-cases.

Inline logic can work initially, but it usually causes duplication and policy drift.

## Roadmap

Start with global logging and error interceptors, then add policy-based guards per module as authorization needs mature.
